Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-14

On-Device Audio and Speech Processing

Takeheed processes all audio and speech transcription directly on your Mac. Audio streams from your microphone or system audio are analyzed in memory and are never transmitted to external servers, cloud providers, or the developer.

Data Sent to External Services

Your audio never leaves your Mac. The only part of what was said that can leave is the words around a mention, and only inside an alert that you explicitly set up yourself. If you enable any of the following optional alert channels, Takeheed sends the alert directly to the service you chose:

  • ntfy: Sends a notification to your chosen ntfy topic on the ntfy server you set (ntfy.sh unless you change it). It holds the word that was heard and the words around it.
  • CallMeBot: Sends a request to trigger a Telegram voice call to you. The message that is read out is yours to set; by default it holds the word that was heard and the words around it.
  • Custom Webhook: Sends a signed JSON payload to the URL you specify (such as Slack, Home Assistant, Zapier, or n8n). It holds the match details, the words around the mention and, when available, up to 30 seconds of what was said before it.

CoolSolve LLP does not operate proxy servers, collect these payloads, or have access to your alert data. Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

Speech Model Download

When your Mac cannot use Apple's on-device speech recognition for your language, or when you choose the local model in Settings, Takeheed downloads a speech model once from huggingface.co: a file of 32 to 60 MB, plus a voice-detection file of under 1 MB. The request asks only for the files. It carries none of your audio, text or settings; like any download, it shows the host your IP address and which file you asked for. After that, transcription works without an internet connection. When Apple's own speech assets are needed, macOS downloads them, not Takeheed.

Purchases

Takeheed offers Pro, a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; CoolSolve LLP never sees your payment details. Takeheed asks the App Store on your Mac whether your Apple Account owns Pro, and keeps the last answer on your Mac so that Pro works from the moment the app opens. No purchase information is sent to CoolSolve LLP or anyone else.

No Accounts, No Tracking, No Analytics

Takeheed requires no account or registration. The app contains no third-party tracking frameworks, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising code. We collect no telemetry about your device or usage.

Local History Storage

Takeheed can maintain a local history of detected mentions and surrounding transcript snippets so you can review what was said. This history is stored strictly on your Mac in application storage. You can search your history, turn off history recording entirely, or delete all records at any time from Settings.

Secure Credential Storage

Any tokens or secrets you provide for optional webhook verification or third-party notification services are stored securely in your macOS Keychain.
