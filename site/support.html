Support and FAQ

Answers to common questions about using Takeheed on your Mac.

Where is the app?

Takeheed is a menu bar app. It does not show an icon in the Dock or open a persistent main window. Look for the ear icon in your macOS menu bar at the top of the screen. Click the ear icon to check status, snooze, pause or resume listening, and open Settings, History, or the Live Transcript window.

Why does it need Screen & System Audio Recording?

To capture audio from video calls, meetings, and playing videos, Takeheed uses Apple's ScreenCaptureKit framework. Takeheed does not capture, display, or store screen video. The video stream is set to a minimal 2×2 pixel size and discarded immediately so that only audio is processed.

macOS asks me every month to re-approve screen recording

In macOS 15 Sequoia and later, macOS shows a monthly system prompt for all apps that capture system audio using ScreenCaptureKit. This is an expected macOS security prompt. Approve the prompt to allow Takeheed to continue listening to call audio.

It alerted on the wrong word

If Takeheed triggers on words that sound similar, open Settings and adjust the rule sensitivity from Loose or Balanced to Exact. You can also specify exact words or phrases so that Takeheed only matches what you entered.

It missed my name

Spoken names may be transcribed phonetically by the speech engine. You can improve detection by adding common variants and nicknames to your rule (for example, "Bala, Balakumar, BK"). You can also switch sensitivity to Balanced or Loose, and open the Live Transcript window to see how words are transcribed in real time.

Older or Intel Macs

On Macs without Apple's newest on-device speech analyzer, Takeheed automatically downloads a small local speech model (~32 to 60 MB). Once downloaded, all transcription runs entirely on your Mac without internet access.

How do I use it with Shortcuts / the URL scheme?

Takeheed provides native actions in the macOS Shortcuts app to start, pause, toggle, snooze, add keywords, and fetch recent mentions.

You can also control Takeheed via the takeheed:// URL scheme:

  • takeheed://start — Start listening
  • takeheed://pause — Pause listening
  • takeheed://toggle — Toggle listening state
  • takeheed://resume — Resume listening
  • takeheed://snooze?minutes=30 — Snooze alerts for specified minutes
  • takeheed://open/settings — Open Settings window
  • takeheed://open/history — Open History window
  • takeheed://open/live — Open Live Transcript window
